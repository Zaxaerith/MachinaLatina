## Illustrative commercial model, not a price forecast
Use a hypothetical constant purchasing-power accounting unit U for arithmetic only; U is not an established fictional currency or an actual dollar. For a PC maker assume fixed development/support preparation F=2,000,000 U; unit net revenue after retail channel discount P=400 U; unit variable production/distribution/support cost V=320 U. Contribution margin m=P-V=80 U. Break-even under these simplified assumptions is 25,000 units. At 10,000 units the operating contribution minus fixed cost is -1,200,000 U; at 50,000 units it is +2,000,000 U. Excludes taxes, finance, inventory, depreciation and changes in marginal cost; do not infer actual DOMESTICA finances. If a professional workstation has fewer buyers it needs larger margins, long-term support income or lower development cost; not inherently inferior technology.

For a game platform illustrative hardware loss 20 U per unit, net platform contribution 5 U per game sold for that console: four games cover hardware loss before fixed platform cost. At two games the contribution is -10 U, at eight games +20 U. No asserted attach rate or legal licensing policy. Platform owner may instead sell hardware profitably; neither model is inevitable.

## Compatibility declaration
A future commercial catalogue should distinguish DATA (explicit profile/codec), SOURCE (language/backend), BINARY (ISA, loader, procedure and OS ABI), VISUAL (font/layout), DEVICE (driver/firmware) and numerical equivalence. A claim must identify the exact version and tests, not use an undifferentiated "Nova compatible" label. These are catalogue fields, not a completed certification standard.
