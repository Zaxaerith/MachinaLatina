# Premise / 世界观与方法

**Status: CANON assumptions; DRAFT consequences.**

## Point of divergence / 分歧点

Classical Latin remains an actively transmitted language of science, law, administration and engineering through the modern age. Its traditional **23-letter orthographic core** survives; there is no *separate* J, U or W. New technical terms, lowercase typography and modern writing conventions can develop without changing that core.

Greek continues to enjoy institutional prestige in mathematics, philosophy, medicine and advanced education. Its script is treated as a first-class requirement for scientific computing, not an optional foreign add-on. This is a **counterfactual continuation** of a historical Greco-Roman bilingual tendency, not a claim that every ancient Roman ranked Greek above Latin.

Physics, semiconductor science, computation theory and economic constraints remain broadly comparable to real history. Particular vendors, standardization bodies and machine designs **need not** be identical.

## Non-negotiable analytical principles / 推演原则

1. **Do not assume ASCII.** Start with 1950s communications and machine-character constraints.
2. **Do not assume English-derived acronyms.** Name engineering concepts in the fictional Latin tradition, but distinguish plausible neologisms from historically attested words.
3. **Distinguish glyphs, characters and bytes.** Latin A and Greek Alpha Α may look identical and still need different character identities.
4. **Model engineering costs.** 6-bit packing, page shifts, printing, transmission, random access, backward compatibility and memory budgets matter.
5. **Model legacy.** New standards need migration paths from earlier fictional standards.
6. **No magical ancient technology.** The divergence is linguistic-cultural and institutional, not an earlier invention of transistors.
7. **Other languages matter.** A bilingual design is not sufficient for worldwide computing.

## What is fixed vs open?

**CANON:** The main line adopts an eight-bit directly encoded Latin–Greek standard in the later 1960s (working title CVL-68); earlier six-bit page-shift machines remain part of the historical lineage.

**DRAFT:** Every specific code-point mapping; the CVL acronym's official historical expansion; machine vendors; file formats; machine opcodes; transliteration and Greek polytonic marks; later universal character encoding.

## Canonicality labels

- HIST: real history, requiring a checkable citation.
- CANON: asserted fact **within this fictional world**, subject to explicit future revision.
- DRAFT: one competing engineering proposal, not normative.

A proposal becomes CANON only when its constraints, alternatives and compatibility consequences are documented in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## 语言学边界

古典拉丁语「持续活用」不等于两千年完全不创造新词。可以出现拉丁语科学新词与借词，但核心拉丁字母仍按古典 23 字母传统书写。希腊语是否完整保留古典语法、是否形成现代通用口语、不同地区是否采用不同书写变体，暂不预设。
