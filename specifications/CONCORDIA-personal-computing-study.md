# CONCORDIA — Personal computing sovereignty and post-desktop systems
> FUTURE SCENARIO / DRAFT · 2026-11-26. Conditional institutional and architecture study, not a legal standard or implemented platform.

## 1. Separate ownership and control
Distinguish device ownership, account/principal identity, document custody, content license, model-weight rights, runtime permission, and payment for remote computation. None implies the others. A local owner may choose compatible software, models and trust roots where hardware and policy permit; another institution can deny use of its resources. A public network is not a common permission domain. Owner-controlled boot follows CVSTOS policy studies, not universal support. Export data with original bytes, declared COS/CVL encoding, schemas, revisions, provenance and applicable rights. Exported model state requires compatible model/runtime and license; personal memory is distinct from weights and task logs. Backup, migration and deletion need explicit storage/retention policies; deletion cannot recall copies already disclosed. Offline manual applications remain available.

## 2. A federation above existing OS families
ORDO-N: personal computers, servers and native processes/files. PARTES: specialized services and scientific records. CVSTOS: mobile/embedded identity and bounded permissions. A CONCORDIA coordinator exposes a task view and adapters, not a new kernel or universal ABI. TABVLA may present a workspace spanning devices, but local windows, precise editing and ordinary files remain useful. MENTIS planners are replaceable; no trusted central personality owns all resources. Independent models can propose competing solutions; disagreement is retained for review, not automatically resolved by majority vote. Tool results are untrusted input to planners.

## 3. Physical network limits
Illustrative task transfers 1 GiB input and 16 MiB output, sequentially, across a path with sustained net 1 Gbit/s in each direction, with remote computation 2 s and two assumed 40 ms round trips. Ideal serialization 8.589934592 + 0.134217728 s; total 10.80415232 s before setup, queueing, retries, storage, verification and conversion. If local computation takes 6 s, remote execution is slower in this example. If input is already authorized and resident remotely, only output serialization and the assumed two round trips plus compute remain (2.214217728 s); this is a different scenario, not free migration of data. Latency, network failure, privacy, energy and price may outweigh compute gains. Optical LVMEN links cannot remove propagation delay, authenticate callers or solve distributed consistency. No measured performance or 2050 forecast.

## 4. Scientific network
ORBIS nodes retain region-specific observations, coordinate/time support, model versions and uncertainty. Federation exchanges only authorized pinned data or derived results. A missing institution or stale sensor produces an explicit gap, not fabricated global state. Distinguish observation, scene, prediction, numerical check and human review. Scientific validity does not follow from remote receipts or agreement among models. RATIO-I remains bounded; higher-precision and approximate device profiles need separate contracts. Infrastructure control excluded.

## 5. Institutional branches
Preferred future: independent interoperable providers, user-controlled local resources, public standards and accountable research cooperation. Alternatives: fragmented vendor islands with proprietary agents; centralized cloud dominance due to economies of scale; slower adoption with local conventional systems dominant. LIBERTAS CODICVM can advocate open interfaces, repairability, export and device-owner choice; cannot assume all model weights or training data are freely licensed. CCL/CSM text standards heritage motivates common identity and multilingual representation without a single global governing authority. No new legal regime, market share, national borders or mandatory licensing assigned.
